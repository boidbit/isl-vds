# Art Direction

## Step 1: Three candidate directions

### A. Soft storybook cartoon
*Picture-book illustration brought to 3D: everything rounded, warm and gentle.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Characters** | Large soft head (about 1/5.5 of body height), long flowing robe that hides the legs, wide bell sleeves, mitten hands. Faceless: a small nose and a sculpted beard give the head a direction. Cloth gets a soft "sheen" so the edges glow. |
| **Dog** | Slender saluki-style desert dog with a deep chest, tucked waist, long floppy ears, big dark eyes with a highlight, and a curved tail. Thirst shows through the silhouette: head low, ears flat, tongue out, ribs showing. |
| **Environment** | Smooth rolling dunes with wind ripples, rounded sandstone boulders with stripes, grass tufts, a broad acacia, distant mesas fading into haze, soft clouds. |
| **Lighting** | Low golden sun, soft long shadows, a warm rim light to separate characters from the background, cool lavender fill in the shadows, gentle bloom. |
| **Palette** | Apricot sand, terracotta, sage green, cream linen, dusty blue sky, lavender shadows. |
| **Pros** | Very appealing and family-friendly. Hides technical limits (no facial rig, simple shapes). Cheap to render. Easy to keep consistent across 100 stories. |
| **Cons** | Can feel too "kiddy" if colours are too saturated. Needs care so serious stories still feel weighty. |

### B. Rich stylized cinematic
*Feature-animation look: dramatic light, depth of field, volumetric haze, detailed textures.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Characters** | Realistic proportions with stylized faces, layered textured costumes, cloth simulation. |
| **Dog** | Fur shading, realistic anatomy, subtle muscle movement. |
| **Environment** | Dense, high-detail sets with sculpted rock, volumetric dust and god rays. |
| **Lighting** | Strong chiaroscuro, volumetrics, heavy colour grading. |
| **Palette** | Deep teal shadows against burnt orange highlights. |
| **Pros** | Most impressive "wow" factor per frame. |
| **Cons** | Needs proper sculpted and rigged assets (Blender/artist work). Very slow to render on CPU/GitHub runners. Realistic faces conflict with the faceless rule. Hard to reuse quickly. |

### C. Simple polished Islamic channel style
*Clean flat-shaded 3D like many Islamic kids' channels: bright, clear and minimal.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Characters** | Simple faceless figures with solid colours and a clear outline. |
| **Dog** | Simple, clean, cute. |
| **Environment** | Sparse backgrounds with flat colour sky and a few props. |
| **Lighting** | Even, bright, almost shadowless. |
| **Palette** | Bright primaries, white, sky blue. |
| **Pros** | Fastest to produce. Very readable on phones. |
| **Cons** | Looks generic, like every other channel. Little emotion or mood. This is close to the "cheap demo" feel we want to leave behind. |

## Step 2: Chosen direction

**A, Soft storybook cartoon, with B's cinematic camera and lighting language.**

| Beauty | ●●●●○ | ●●●●● | ●●○○○ |
| Emotional appeal | ●●●●● | ●●●●○ | ●●○○○ |
| Reusability (code-built assets) | ●●●●● | ●○○○○ | ●●●●● |
| Render performance | ●●●●○ | ●○○○○ | ●●●●● |
| Islamic storytelling fit (faceless, gentle) | ●●●●● | ●●○○○ | ●●●●○ |

The shapes stay simple and rounded, so everything can still be generated by code and rendered on a normal CPU.
The quality comes from things that cost little: **light** (low golden sun, rim light, lavender shadows),
**camera** (low angles, depth of field, slow push-ins), **atmosphere** (haze, dust in the light, distant layers)
and **silhouette** (robe shape, dog posture).

## Style rules (apply to every story)

1. **No hard facets.** Every surface uses smooth normals. Characters are built from rounded profiles (lathe), never boxes.
2. **Cloth has sheen.** Robes, turbans and fur use a soft sheen so edges catch the light.
3. **Three-light setup.** Warm key sun with soft shadows, warm rim light from behind, cool sky fill. No flat lighting.
4. **Shadows are lavender, not grey.** The fill light tints shadows cool so the warm light reads as warm.
5. **Always layer depth.** Foreground (grass, rock), subject, midground (well, tree), background (dunes, mesas in haze), sky (clouds, sun glow).
6. **Depth of field on every shot.** Focus on the subject, soft background.
7. **Pose before detail.** Emotion comes from head angle, spine curve and hand position: the thirsty man slumps, the humble man kneels and bows, the thirsty dog keeps its head low with ears flat.
8. **Faceless humans.** A small nose and the beard shape only. No eyes or mouths on human characters. Never depict Allah, Prophets, angels or named Companions.
9. **Warm palette.** Sand `#E8B98A`, terracotta `#B5643C`, linen `#EFE4D0`, sage `#8C9A5B`, sky `#9FC3DD`, shadow lavender `#8C7FA8`.
10. **Grade at the end.** Gentle contrast, slight warmth, vignette, cinematic letterbox.

## Implementation plan

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Render pipeline | Post-processing: ambient occlusion (N8AO), depth of field focused on the subject, soft bloom, colour grade, vignette. Soft shadow maps. |
| Materials | `src/library/style.ts`: shared palette plus cloth (sheen), skin, fur, stone and sand materials. |
| Human | Rebuilt with lathe-profiled robe (with fabric folds at the hem), bell sleeves, wrapped turban, shawl, mitten hands. The robe "pools" on the ground when kneeling. |
| Dog | Rebuilt as a slender saluki-type dog with expressive head, eyes with highlights, floppy ears, tongue and ribs. |
| Desert | Rippled sand (procedural normal map), layered far dunes, distant mesas in haze, painted sky with soft clouds, floating dust motes. |
| Props | Rounded-stone well on a sand mound, sandstone boulders, grass tufts, a sculpted acacia, date palms. |
| Camera | New `custom` shot (exact position and look-at with smooth start-to-end move) for hand-composed hero shots, plus DOF focus on a named actor. |
| Benchmark | `stories/benchmark-pilana.json`: the emotional peak of the story, the man giving water to the dog, as one composed 8-second shot. |

## 2D Shorts: shot rules (apply to every film)

1. **Every shot shows a character.** Viewers connect to people, not objects. Tools, pots, water and coins are
   framed *with* the person holding or looking at them.
2. **Object-only inserts are the exception:** at most ~1 second, and the very next shot is the character's face/reaction.
3. **Two characters in one frame are both sharp.** Depth blur only on far background layers.
4. **Camera stays close:** medium or closer for dialogue; wide shots only to open a new place or to end.
5. **Hook in the first 2 seconds** with the character doing something (not a title card).
6. **Characters below the vertical middle, captions below them** (9:16): faces never covered by text.

## 2D look: our own, and free to change

The first 2D films were built from a reference screenshot of another YouTube channel. That was a starting
point, **not a style to copy forever**. Our look should be our own (better for the channel's identity, and it
keeps us clear of copying another creator).

- Per film, pick a look that fits the story and say it in one line in the film's tsx header, e.g.
  "warm storybook, thick outline" / "soft painted, no outline, dusk palette" / "paper cut-out with texture".
- Things that may change from film to film: palette and grade, outline weight (thick, thin, none), shading
  (flat, 2-tone, soft gradient), textures (paper grain, paint strokes), sky and light style, proportions
  (bigger heads for kids' stories, taller figures for serious ones), caption font and colour.
- Things that stay: readable shapes at phone size, faces clear in close-ups, Islamic rules, captions at 75%
  height, warm light for kindness and cold light for danger.
- Reuse the same assets with a new look through props (colour/outline/texture) rather than new files when possible.
