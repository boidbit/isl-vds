# Pyase Kutte Ki Kahani

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 2363

## Scene 1 — Tapta registan (00–05 sec)
**Environment:** Sunehri ret ke teele, tez dhoop, door ek purana patthar ka kuan aur ek hara-bhara ped.
**Action:** Musafir thake hue kadmon se chalta hai. Apne maathe par haath rakhta hai aur door kuan dekhkar uski taraf badhta hai.
**Camera:** Wide establishing shot, followed by a smooth side-tracking shot. Camera dheere-dheere character ke kareeb aaye.
**Narration:** "Ek baar ek musafir pyaas se behaal tha. Use ek kuan mila."
**Sound:** Registani hawa aur ret par kadmon ki awaaz.

## Scene 2 — Paani ki talaash (05–09 sec)
**Environment:** Kuen ke andar thande rang, patthar ki deewarein aur chamakta hua paani.
**Action:** Musafir kuen mein utarta hai, dono haathon se paani peeta hai aur wapas bahar nikalta hai.
**Camera:** Top-down shot into the well, followed by a close-up of hands touching water. Quick cinematic cut to the man climbing out.
**Narration:** "Woh neeche utra aur paani peeker bahar aaya."
**Sound:** Paani ki halki chhap-chhap.

## Scene 3 — Pyasa kutta (09–14 sec)
**Environment:** Kuen ke paas geeli mitti aur tez dhoop.
**Action:** Ek bhure rang ka kutta haanf raha hai. Uske kaan jhuke hue hain aur woh geeli mitti chaat raha hai. Musafir use dekhkar ruk jaata hai.
**Camera:** Ground-level emotional close-up of the dog. Rack focus from the dog to the man standing behind it.
**Narration:** "Wahan ek kutta pyaas se tadapkar geeli mitti chaat raha tha."
**Sound:** Kutte ki haanfne ki awaaz.

## Scene 4 — Rehamdili (14–21 sec)
**Action:** Musafir dobara kuen mein utarta hai. Apne chamde ke joote mein paani bharta hai, use daanton se pakadta hai aur upar chadhta hai.
**Camera:** Over-the-shoulder shot looking into the well. Close-up of the shoe filling with water. Low-angle shot of the man climbing upward.
**Narration:** "Usne dobara kuen mein utarkar apne joote mein paani bhara."
**Sound:** Paani ki boondein aur pattharon par haathon ki awaaz.

## Scene 5 — Neki (21–25 sec)
**Action:** Musafir ghutnon par baithkar joote ko kutte ke saamne rakhta hai. Kutta paani peeta hai. Uski poonch dheere-dheere hilne lagti hai.
**Camera:** Side-profile medium shot, followed by an emotional close-up of the dog drinking water. Slow cinematic camera movement.
**Narration:** "Aur us bejubaan kutte ko paani pilaya."
**Sound:** Paani peene ki awaaz aur halki hawa.

## Scene 6 — Khoobsurat anjaam (25–30 sec)
**Environment:** Sunehri roshni, shaant registan aur khoobsurat aasman.
**Action:** Musafir kutte ke paas se uthkar apne safar par aage badhta hai. Kutta sukoon se ped ke neeche baith jaata hai.
**Camera:** Slow cinematic pull-back, revealing the man, dog, well and beautiful desert landscape. Smooth fade-out.
**Narration:** "Allah ko uski ye neki itni pasand aayi ki uske gunah maaf farma diye."
**Screen text:** Kisi bhi neki ko chhota mat samjho. — Sahih al-Bukhari 2363
