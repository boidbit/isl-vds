# Samundar Mein 1,000 Dinar (owner's script)

TITLE: Samundar Mein 1,000 Dinar
SOURCE: Sahih al-Bukhari 2291
HADITH KA MATLAB: “Abu Huraira se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne bataya ke Bani Israil ke ek aadmi ne doosre se hazaar dinar qarz maange. Usne gawah maange, toh qarz maangne wale ne kaha ke Allah gawah ke taur par kaafi hai. Usne zamaanat maangi, toh qarz maangne wale ne kaha ke Allah zamaanat ke liye kaafi hai. Doosra raazi hua aur ek tay muddat ke liye qarz de diya. Qarz lene wala samundar paar gaya. Kaam khatam hua toh usne waqt par lautne ke liye kashti dhoondhi, magar use kashti na mili. Usne lakdi ka ek tukda khokhla kiya, usmein hazaar dinar aur qarz dene wale ke naam ek khat rakha aur use band kar diya. Kinare par usne dua ki. Usne qarz, gawahi aur zamaanat ka zikr kiya aur kaha ke poori koshish ke baad bhi kashti nahi mili, isliye woh paise Allah ke supurd karta hai. Usne lakdi samundar mein phenk di aur kashti ki talaash jaari rakhi. Qarz dene wala kinare par dekhne aaya ke koi kashti uske paise laayi hai ya nahi. Use lakdi dikhi aur woh jalane ke liye ghar le gaya. Kaati toh andar se paise aur khat mila. Baad mein qarz lene wala khud hazaar dinar lekar aaya aur bataya ke isse pehle use koi kashti na mili. Qarz dene wale ne poochha ke kya usne kuch bheja tha. Usne phir kashti na milne ka zikr kiya. Qarz dene wale ne kaha ke Allah ne lakdi mein bheje hue paise uski taraf se pahuncha diye, isliye woh apne hazaar dinar le jaaye.”

CHARACTER DESIGN
Qarz lene wala: Taqreeban 35 saal, ret-rang kurta, neela kandhe ka kapda, chhoti daadhi. Talaash mein bechain. Paise lautate waqt sanjeeda. (tasawwur)
Qarz dene wala: Taqreeban 50 saal, zaitooni kurta, bhoori shawl, daadhi mein safed lakeerein. Lakdi khulne par aankhen hairat se thehar jaati hain. (tasawwur)
Background characters: Tokri wala raahgir, ek kashti wala aur samundari parinde. (tasawwur)
Frame: 9:16 vertical, 2D cartoon. Qarz dene wale ke kinaare par pathar ki seedhiyaan aur mitti-rang ghar. Qarz lene wala jis kinaare par gaya, wahan lakdi ka ghaat aur neela-surmai paani. Lakdi ke tukde par ek tedhi gaanth rakho jo dono taraf se pehchaani jaaye. (tasawwur)

SCENE 1 — Aari ke neeche (00–03 sec)
Surroundings: Chhota aangan. Takhtay par rakhi lakdi, paas mein jalane ke liye ikattha ki gayi lakdiyaan.
Main action: Pehle frame se aari chalti hai. Do second ke andar chire se dinar ki chamak nazar aati hai.
Background activity: Aari ka burada girta hai, kapde ka parda hilta hai, deewar par shaakh ka saya dolta hai.
Camera: Lakdi ka extreme close-up. Chamak par cut karke qarz dene wale ka frontal close-up. Hairat mein ek pal seedha lens par dekhe.
Lighting: Chire par dhoop ki patli lakeer.
Narration: “Lakdi mein hazaar dinar?”
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Aari ki khar-khar, phir achanak khamoshi.
(tasawwur: pehle manzar ki jhalak, aangan, lakdi ki gaanth aur dinar par chamak)

SCENE 2 — Tay waqt ka qarz (03–18 sec)
Surroundings: Mitti-rang ghar ka aangan. Khule darwaze ke paar pathar ki seedhiyaan paani tak utarti hain.
Main action: Kahani qarz lene ke waqt par lautti hai. Gawah aur zamaanat ki baat ke baad qarz dene wala raazi hota hai aur thaili mez par aage sarkaata hai.
Background activity: Seedhiyon par ek raahgir utarta hai, rassi par sookhta kapda hilta hai, neeche paani pathar se takraata hai.
Camera: Eye-level wide mein ghar aur seedhiyaan pehchaan mein aa jaayen. Phir mez tak push-in aur qarz dene wale ke faisle par frontal close-up.
Lighting: Mitti-rang deewaron se lautti garam dhoop.
Narration: “Qissa Bani Israil ke do aadmiyon ka hai. Ek ne doosre se hazaar dinar udhaar maange.”
Narration: “Gawah aur zamaanat ki baat aayi, toh usne kaha ke dono ke liye Allah kaafi hai. Doosre ne qarz de diya.”
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Seedhiyon par qadam aur thaili ki dabi khanak.
(tasawwur: jagah, kapde ki thaili, chehron ke reaction aur background)

SCENE 3 — Wapas jaane wali kashti (18–35 sec)
Surroundings: Samundar ke paar lakdi ka ghaat. Wapas jaane ki taraf door tak khula paani.
Main action: Pehle kinaare ki pathar wali seedhiyaan dikhao. Samundar paar jaati kashti ka chhota shot, phir waqt aage badhao. Ab kaam khatam ho chuka hai. Aadmi lakdi ke ghaat ke sire par aa kar wapas jaane wali kashti talaashta hai.
Background activity: Ghaat ke neeche lehrein uthti hain, dheeli rassi khambe se lagti hai, samundari parinda paani ke upar se guzarta hai.
Camera: Safar ka wide shot. Phir ghaat par low-angle tracking se aadmi ke saath aage jao. Uske POV mein khaali paani, phir bechaini par saamne se close-up.
Lighting: Kashti ke safar mein khuli dhoop. Talaash ke waqt baadalon se chhanti feeki roshni.
Narration: “Qarz lene wala apne kaam se samundar paar gaya. Kaam khatam hua toh use tay waqt par qarz lautane ke liye pehle kinaare par wapas jaana tha.”
Narration: “Bahut dhoondha, lekin wapas jaane wali kashti nahi mili.”
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Safar mein paani kashti se takraaye. Ghaat par sirf hawa, rassi aur lehron ki awaaz.
(tasawwur: safar ka shot, ghaat, rassi, roshni aur talaash ki visual staging)

SCENE 4 — Lakdi mein khat (35–50 sec)
Surroundings: Lakdi ke ghaat se zara hatkar chapte pathar. Ek pathar par dinar aur moda hua khat.
Main action: Aadmi lakdi khokhli karta hai, dinar aur khat andar rakhta hai aur munh band karta hai. Kinare par lakdi sambhale dua karta hai, phir use samundar mein phenk deta hai.
Background activity: Lakdi ke chhilke hawa mein sarakte hain, jhaag pathron mein ghusta hai, ghaat ke upar ek parinda rukh badalta hai.
Camera: Top-down insert mein dinar aur khat paas paas. Dua mein aadmi ka saamne se close-up. Lakdi paani par gire toh camera satah ki oonchai se uske saath chale.
Lighting: Lakdi par garam dhoop, paani par thandi neeli roshni.
Narration: “Usne lakdi ka ek tukda khokhla kiya. Andar hazaar dinar aur qarz dene wale ke naam ek khat rakha.”
Narration: “Lakdi band ki, dua mein paise Allah ke supurd kiye aur lakdi samundar mein phenk di.”
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Auzaar ki thak, dinaron ki khanak aur phir paani mein girne ki chhap.
(tasawwur: pathar par lakdi rakhna, khat ka roop, dua ke waqt pose aur background)

SCENE 5 — Ek hi samundar, do kinaare (50–61 sec)
Surroundings: Pehle lakdi ka ghaat. Phir wohi pathar ki seedhiyaan jo qarz ke scene mein ghar ke paas dikhi thi.
Main action: Qarz lene wala ghaat par ab bhi kashti ki talaash mein hai. Cut. Qarz dene wala apne kinaare par seedhiyaan utar kar door samundar ki taraf dekhta hai. Neeche frame mein lakdi ki tedhi gaanth ubharti hai.
Background activity: Ghaat ki rassi dolti hai, pathar ki seedhi se parinda udta hai, ek lehar lakdi ko palat deti hai.
Camera: Dono wide shots ko lehar uthne ki harkat par jodo. Qarz dene wale ki nazar ka peecha karte hue camera door paani se neeche lakdi tak laaye.
Lighting: Ghaat par feeki chhaon. Pathar ki seedhiyon par garam, narm dhoop.
Narration: “Woh phir bhi kashti dhoondhta raha.”
Narration: “Udhar, doosre kinaare par qarz dene wala dekhne aaya ke koi kashti uske paise laayi hai ya nahi.”
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Dono shots ke beech lehron ki awaaz chalti rahe.
(tasawwur: dono kinaaron ke beech cuts, seedhiyaan aur lakdi palatne ki harkat)

SCENE 6 — Dinar aur wohi khat (61–71 sec)
Surroundings: Kinara, phir pehle shot wala aangan aur takhta.
Main action: Qarz dene wala lakdi utha kar ghar laata hai. Wahi aari wala shot lauto. Is baar lakdi poori khulti hai. Uske haath mein wohi moda hua khat dikhta hai.
Background activity: Baruda dheere baithta hai, parde ka kona uthta hai, kabootar munder par phudakta hai.
Camera: Medium shot se aari par cut. Focus dinaron se khat par lao. Phir saamne se aadmi ka close-up. Uski nazar khat se uth kar ek pal lens par aaye.
Lighting: Chire ke andhere mein dinaron aur khat par dhoop ki patli lakeer.
Narration: “Use kinare par lakdi dikhi. Woh jalane ke liye ghar le gaya.”
Narration: “Jab aari chalayi, lakdi ke andar dinar mile. Aur saath wohi khat.”
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Shuru wali khar-khar. Aari rukne par aadha second khamoshi, phir kaagaz ki sar-sar.
(tasawwur: lakdi khulne ki framing, khat haath mein lene ka shot aur reaction)

SCENE 7 — Woh khud aa gaya (71–82 sec)
Surroundings: Pehle kinaare ki pathar wali seedhiyaan aur upar qarz dene wale ka ghar.
Main action: Is baar kashti waqai seedhiyon ke paas lagti hai. Qarz lene wala utarta hai aur ghar pahunchkar doosre hazaar dinar mez par rakh deta hai. Khat pehle se wahin pada hai.
Background activity: Kashti paani par halki jhoolti hai, kashti wala rassi kheenchta hai, upar aangan ka parda hawa mein hilta hai.
Camera: Paani ki satah se kashti ka low-angle shot. Thaili rakhne par usi framing mein cut jisme qarz diya gaya tha. Qarz lene wale ka saamne se close-up, bechain nazar ek pal seedha lens par.
Lighting: Seedhiyon aur khule darwaze mein narm dhoop.
Narration: “Baad mein qarz lene wale ko kashti mil gayi. Woh khud bhi hazaar dinar lekar aa pahuncha.”
Narration: “Qarz dene wale ne poochha, ‘Kya tumne mere paas kuch bheja tha?’”
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Kashti ka pathar se halka lagna. Phir thaili mein sikkon ki khanak.
(tasawwur: kashti lagne ka manzar, mez par khat ki jagah aur thaili rakhne ka shot)

AAKHRI SCENE: Seekh aur end card (82–94 sec)
Surroundings: Wahi aangan. Mez par ek taraf lakdi se nikla khat, doosri taraf abhi laayi hui thaili.
Main action: Qarz dene wala khat ko chhoo kar thaili wapas sarkaata hai. Qarz lene wale ki ungliyan thaili pakadti hain. Uske chehre se bechaini utarti hai. 90 sec par end card aaye.
Background activity: Parda aahista hilta hai, shaakh ka saya mez par sarakta hai, munder se parinde udte hain.
Camera: Khat aur thaili ka ek hi insert. Phir saamne se qarz lene wale ki aankhon par close-up, nazar ek lamhe ke liye seedhi lens par. End card ke liye camera dheere peeche aaye.
Lighting: Aangan ki garam, narm roshni.
Narration: “Rasoolullah ﷺ ne bataya ke qarz dene wale ne usse kaha, ‘Allah ne lakdi mein bheje hue paise tumhari taraf se pahuncha diye. Apne hazaar dinar le jao.’”
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Mez par thaili sarkti hai. Door ki lehron par khatam karo.
(tasawwur: khat chhoona, thaili sarkana, chehre ka reaction aur end card)
