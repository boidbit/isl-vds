# Billi aur Aurat (Sahih al-Bukhari 2365)

CHARACTER DESIGN

Aurat: 35–40 saal ki, maroon rang ka lamba libaas, beige headscarf, brown footwear, expressive cartoon face.

Billi: Chhoti narangi aur safed rang ki billi, badi aankhein, mulayam fur, expressive kaan aur poonch.

Background characters: Dukandaar, buzurg, khelte bachche, paani lekar jaati auratein aur gaon ke musafir.

SCENE 1 — Powerful Hook (00–04 sec)

Surroundings: Purani Arabian basti ka chhota sa aangan. Mitti ki deewarein, lakdi ka darwaza, kone mein paani ke matke aur deewar par latakti laalten.

Main action: Ek lakdi ke pinjre ke andar billi baithi hai. Woh apna panja bahar nikaalti hai aur kamzor awaaz mein miaow karti hai.

Background activity: Deewar par ek chidiya aakar baithti hai. Khajoor ke ped ki parchhai hawa ke saath hilti hai. Gali se ek musafir guzarta hai.

Camera: Extreme close-up of the cat's eyes. Slowly pull back to reveal the wooden cage.

Lighting: Pinjre ke andar andhera, bahar chamakti dhoop.

Narration:
"Kya tum jaante ho, ek billi ki wajah se ek aurat ko jahannam ki saza mili?"

Sound: Billi ki miaow aur halki suspenseful ambience.

SCENE 2 — Billi ki qaid (04–10 sec)

Surroundings: Ghar ka khula aangan. Farsh par purane patthar, deewar ke paas lakdi ke sandook, rassi par sookhte kapde aur khidki ke paas mitti ke bartan.

Main action: Aurat billi ko pinjre mein band karke darwaza laga deti hai. Billi bahar nikalne ki koshish karti hai, lekin aurat wahan se chali jaati hai.

Background activity: Khuli gali mein do bachche khel rahe hain. Ek dukandaar tokri mein khajoor saja raha hai. Ek gadha samaan lekar guzarta hai. Kapde hawa mein lehra rahe hain.

Camera: Medium side shot of the woman, close-up of the cage latch, followed by a low-angle shot from the cat's perspective.

Narration:
"Ek aurat ne ek billi ko qaid kar diya aur use azaad nahi kiya."

Sound: Lakdi ka darwaza band hone ki awaaz, bachchon ki halki awaaz aur bazaar ka mahaul.

SCENE 3 — Bhookh aur pyaas (10–18 sec)

Surroundings: Ghar ke andar mitti ka chulha, lakdi ki mez, rotiyan, phalon ki tokri aur paani ka bhara hua matka. Khule darwaze se billi ka pinjra nazar aa raha hai.

Main action: Aurat apne liye khana taiyar karti hai. Doosri taraf billi apne khaali katore ko panje se hilati hai. Woh ummeed bhari nazron se aurat ki taraf dekhti hai, lekin use khana nahi milta.

Background activity: Chulhe se dhuan uth raha hai. Khidki ke bahar parinde udte hain. Gali mein ek aurat sir par paani ka matka lekar guzarti hai.

Camera: Close-up of freshly cooked bread, smooth pan towards the cat's empty bowl, then an emotional ground-level shot of the cat.

Lighting: Ghar ke andar warm orange roshni, pinjre ke paas thandi aur feeki lighting.

Narration:
"Na usne billi ko khana diya, na paani... aur na hi use chhoda, taaki woh khud kuch kha sake."

Sound: Chulhe ki awaaz, bartanon ki khanak aur billi ki dheemi miaow.

SCENE 4 — Guzarta hua waqt (18–26 sec)

Surroundings: Wahi aangan, lekin waqt tezi se badalta hai. Subah ki dhoop se shaam, shaam se andheri raat aur phir subah.

Main action: Billi pehle pinjre mein idhar-udhar chalti hai. Phir dheere-dheere kamzor hokar baith jaati hai. Uske kaan jhuk jaate hain aur harkatein kam ho jaati hain.

Background activity: Suraj ki parchhai deewaron par badalti hai. Gali ke dukandaar apni dukaan band karte hain. Log ghar lautte hain. Raat mein khidkiyon mein diye jalte hain. Subah parinde dobara aane lagte hain.

Camera: Locked cinematic time-lapse shot followed by a slow push-in towards the exhausted cat.

Lighting: Golden daylight → orange sunset → blue moonlight → pale morning light.

Narration:
"Waqt guzarta gaya. Bechari billi bhookh aur pyaas se kamzor hoti gayi."

Sound: Hawa, raat ke keede aur dheere-dheere kam hoti billi ki awaaz.

SCENE 5 — Ek dardnaak anjaam (26–32 sec)

Surroundings: Subah ka waqt. Aangan mein sannata hai. Farsh par sukhe patte pade hain. Pinjre ke bahar khaali katora rakha hai.

Main action: Billi pinjre ke andar aankhein band kiye leti hai. Camera billi ke bajay dheere-dheere khaali katore par focus karta hai.

Background activity: Ek sukha patta hawa se udkar pinjre ke paas girta hai. Deewar par baithi chidiya ud jaati hai. Door gali mein log apne rozmarra ke kaam kar rahe hain.

Camera: Close-up of the empty bowl. Slow rack focus towards the motionless cat. Gradual fade to black.

Narration:
"Aakhirkar woh bejubaan billi bhookh se mar gayi."

Sound: Halki hawa. Narration ke baad ek second ka sannata.

Important: Billi ki maut ko graphic ya disturbing tareeke se mat dikhana.

SCENE 6 — Sakht saza aur seekh (32–45 sec)

Visual: Black background par Hadith ka reference ubhar kar aaye. Iske baad ek symbolic contrast scene dikhaya jaye, jisme ek bachcha khuli gali mein ek billi ke saamne paani ka katora rakhta hai.

Surroundings: Khoobsurat basti, sunehri dhoop, khajoor ke ped, rang-birange kapde aur mitti ke ghar.

Background activity: Bachche khel rahe hain, parinde ud rahe hain, dukandaar samaan bech rahe hain aur pedon ke patte hawa mein hil rahe hain.

Main action: Billi paani peeti hai. Bachcha uske paas baithkar pyaar se use dekhta hai.

Camera: Water bowl close-up, followed by a smooth cinematic pull-back revealing the entire lively village.

Narration:
"Rasoolullah ﷺ ne bataya ki is zulm ki wajah se us aurat ko jahannam ki saza mili. Yaad rakho, bejubaan jaanwaron par reham karna bhi hamari zimmedari hai."

Final screen text:
Bejubaan hain, bejaan nahi.

Sahih al-Bukhari — 2365
