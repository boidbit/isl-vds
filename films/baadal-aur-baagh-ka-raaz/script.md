# Baadal Aur Baagh Ka Raaz (owner's script)

TITLE: Baadal Aur Baagh Ka Raaz
SOURCE: Sahih Muslim 2984a
HADITH KA MATLAB: "Abu Huraira se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya ke ek aadmi sunsaan ilaake mein tha. Usne ek baadal mein awaaz suni, ‘Falan shakhs ke baagh ko paani do.’ Baadal ek taraf gaya aur pathreeli zameen par apna saara paani barsa diya. Wahan ki ek naali mein saara paani aa gaya. Aadmi paani ke peeche chala. Usne dekha ke ek aadmi apne baagh mein kudal se paani ka rukh badal raha tha. Usne poochha, ‘Allah ke bande, tumhara naam kya hai?’ Baagh ke maalik ne apna naam bataya. Yeh wahi naam tha jo usne baadal mein suna tha. Maalik ne poochha, ‘Allah ke bande, tum mera naam kyun poochhte ho?’ Usne kaha, ‘Jis baadal ka yeh paani hai, maine usmein awaaz suni ke falan shakhs ke baagh ko paani do. Wahi naam jo tumhara hai. Tum is baagh mein kya karte ho?’ Maalik ne kaha, ‘Jab tumne yeh baat batayi hai, toh suno. Main dekhta hoon is baagh se kitni paidawaar hoti hai. Uska ek tihai sadaqa kar deta hoon, ek tihai main aur mere ghar wale khaate hain, aur ek tihai main baagh mein wapas laga deta hoon.’"

CHARACTER DESIGN
Musafir: Taqreeban 30 saal, retilay rang ka kurta aur neela kandhe ka kapda. Pehle hairat, phir raaz jaanne ki bechaini. (tasawwur: umar, libaas aur expressions)
Baagh ka maalik: Taqreeban 45 saal, zaitooni kurta aur surmai pagdi. Halki daadhi wala chehra. Baat sunte waqt hairat, jawaab dete waqt sukoon. (tasawwur: umar, libaas aur expressions)
Background characters: Do gaureya aur ek titli. (tasawwur)

SCENE 1 — Awaaz (00–04 sec)
Surroundings: 9:16 vertical 2D frame mein pathreela maidan aur upar ek baadal.
Main action: Pehle frame mein musafir ka qadam beech mein rukta hai. Woh chonk kar baadal ki taraf sar uthaata hai. Hook ki narration pehle do second mein bolo.
Background activity: Hawa mein dhool sarakti hai, sukhi ghaas jhukti hai, do parinde frame paar karte hain.
Camera: Low-angle medium shot se jhatke ka cut saamne se close-up par. Hairat ke pal mein musafir seedha lens mein dekhe.
Lighting: Maidan par tez dhoop aur musafir ke chehre par baadal ki halki chhaon.
Narration: "Baadal se awaaz aayi?"
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Hawa aur bajri par rukte qadam ki awaaz.
(tasawwur: hook, qadam ka rukna, chehre ki hairat aur background)

SCENE 2 — Baagh ka naam (04–13 sec)
Surroundings: Wahi sunsaan maidan aur khula aasman.
Main action: Musafir nazar utha kar baadal dekhta hai. Jo awaaz usne suni, uska matlab narrator batata hai.
Background activity: Baadal dheere sarakta hai, kandhe ka kapda phadphadata hai, ghaas ki pattiyan jhoolti hain.
Camera: Musafir ka POV wide shot. Baadal frame ke upar se beech ki taraf aaye. Phir saamne se musafir ke chehre par slow push-in.
Lighting: Mitti par garam dhoop aur sarakte baadal ki feeki chhaon.
Narration: "Socho, ek aadmi sunsaan ilaake mein tha."
Narration: "Use baadal se awaaz sunai di ke falan shakhs ke baagh ko paani do."
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Hawaa ka dheema jhonka.
(tasawwur: musafir ki khade hone ki jagah, aasman ki rangat aur background ki harkat)

SCENE 3 — Baarish ka rasta (13–21 sec)
Surroundings: Pathreeli zameen aur usmein bani patli naali.
Main action: Baadal se baarish shuru hoti hai. Chhoti dhaarein pathron ke beech se nikal kar ek naali mein milti hain.
Background activity: Boondein pathron se uchhalti hain, ek sukha patta paani par behta hai, ghaas baarish mein jhukti hai.
Camera: Baadal aur maidan ka wide shot. Tilt down karke naali ke kareeb aao, phir zameen ki satah se paani ke saath track karo.
Lighting: Baarish ki narm, surmai roshni. Paani par chhoti chamak.
Narration: "Aur phir baadal aage badha."
Narration: "Paani pathreeli zameen par barsa, aur saara paani ek naali mein ja mila."
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Pathron par tez boondein, phir behti naali.
(tasawwur: naali ki shakl, boondon ka close-up, sukha patta aur background)

SCENE 4 — Baagh tak (21–30 sec)
Surroundings: Naali baagh ke kinare tak pahunchti hai. Andar darakhton ki qatarein hain.
Main action: Musafir paani ka rasta pakad kar baagh tak aata hai. Baagh ka maalik kudal se paani ka rukh mod raha hai.
Background activity: Naali mein paani behta hai, shaakhon ke patte hilte hain, ek gaureya kinare par phudakti hai.
Camera: Musafir ka low-angle medium tracking shot. Uske qadam se pull-back karke wide mein baagh aur kudal chalata maalik dikhao.
Lighting: Baagh ke andar patton se chhan kar roshni padti hai.
Narration: "Woh paani ke saath saath chala."
Narration: "Aage baagh ka maalik kudal se paani ka rukh badal raha tha."
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Geeli mitti mein kudal aur naali ki kal-kal.
(tasawwur: darakhton ki qatarein, chalne ki chaal, roshni aur background)

SCENE 5 — Wahi naam (30–37 sec)
Surroundings: Baagh ki naali ke kinare dono aadmi.
Main action: Musafir sawaal karta hai aur maalik apna naam batata hai. Musafir ki aankhen hairat se khul jaati hain. Sawaal aur jawaab narration se samjhao.
Background activity: Naali ka paani mod ke paas ghoomta hai, ek titli patton ke upar se guzarti hai, musafir ka kapda hawa mein hilta hai.
Camera: Eye-level medium two-shot se cut. Naam pehchaanne ke pal par musafir ka saamne se close-up, seedha lens mein dekhta hua.
Lighting: Chehre par patton ki chhan-chhan roshni.
Narration: "Usne maalik ka naam poochha."
Narration: "Naam wahi tha jo usne baadal se suna tha."
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Paas se behta paani. Naam pehchaanne ke pal par hawa ki awaaz dheemi ho jaaye.
(tasawwur: kirdaron ki jagah, musafir ki hairat, roshni aur background)

SCENE 6 — Teen hisse (37–47 sec)
Surroundings: Baagh ki wohi qatarein aur paas ke darakht ki chhaon.
Main action: Musafir baadal wali awaaz aur apne sawaal ka zikr karta hai. Maalik uski baat sunkar apni paidawaar ke teen hisse samjhata hai. Baat narrator sunaaye.
Background activity: Shaakhon ke saaye sarakte hain, boondein patton se tapakti hain, naali mein patta ghoomta hai.
Camera: Musafir ke kandhe ke upar se medium shot. Maalik ki hairat par saamne se close-up. Jawaab dene ka pal aate hi halka push-in, maalik ki nazar ek lamhe ke liye lens par.
Lighting: Pichhle frame ki feeki chhaon se chehre par narm roshni.
Narration: "Usne maalik ko awaaz ka qissa sunaya aur poochha ke woh baagh mein kya karta hai."
Narration: "Jawaab mein maalik ne teen hisse bataye."
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Patton se girti boondein aur baagh ki hawa.
(tasawwur: chehron ke expressions, baarish ki bachi boondein aur background)

AAKHRI SCENE: Seekh aur end card (47–60 sec)
Surroundings: Baagh mein chatai par paidawaar ki teen tokriyan.
Main action: Teen hisson ka matlab samjhane ke liye tokriyon par bari bari focus aaye. 56 sec par end line aur uske neeche chhota reference dikhayein.
Background activity: Paani naali mein behta rahe, darakht ke patte jhoolein, ek gaureya pichhli shaakh par phudke.
Camera: Halki oonchai se medium shot mein teenon tokriyan. Dheere pull-back karte waqt upar end card ke liye jagah rakho.
Lighting: Darakhton ke beech se narm dhoop aur tokriyon ke neeche halki chhaon.
Narration: "Rasoolullah ﷺ ne bataya ke baagh ka maalik ek tihai sadaqa deta, ek tihai ghar walon ke saath khaata aur ek tihai baagh mein lagata tha."
Dialogue (sirf source se): —
Sound: Naali ki dheemi awaaz aur parindon ki chahchahat.
(tasawwur: tokriyon ka manzar, chatai, background, end line aur comment ka sawaal)

## Owner ka final narration (yahi film mein use hua)
Baadal se awaaz aayi.

Aur us awaaz mein ek naam tha.

Socho, ek aadmi sunsaan ilaake mein tha. Tabhi use ek baadal se awaaz sunai di, “Falan shakhs ke baagh ko paani do.”

Phir baadal aage sarak gaya. Baarish pathreeli zameen par hui aur saara paani ek hi naali mein ja mila. Aadmi us paani ke peeche chal pada.

Aage ek baagh tha. Uska maalik kudal se paani ka rukh mod raha tha. Aadmi ne uska naam poochha.

Wahi naam… jo usne baadal se suna tha.

Maalik ne poochha, “Tum mera naam kyun poochh rahe ho?”

Aadmi ne kaha, “Jis baadal ka yeh paani hai, usi mein maine tumhara naam suna. Tum is baagh mein kya karte ho?”

Rasoolullah ﷺ ne bataya ke maalik apni paidawaar ke teen hisse karta tha.

Ek tihai woh sadaqa kar deta tha.

Ek tihai woh aur uske ghar wale khaate the.

Aur ek tihai woh baagh mein wapas laga deta tha.

Jitna apne ghar ke liye rakhta tha… utna hi sadaqe mein de deta tha.
