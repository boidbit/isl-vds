# Teen Aadmi, Ek Imtihaan (script written with story-craft, confirmed by the owner)

SOURCE: Sahih al-Bukhari 3464 (full text in source.md) - https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3464
STRUCTURE: three tries + mirror (each man sees his own past). The angel is never shown in any form: the camera is
his point of view, the men answer into the lens; his words are told by the narrator.

1. Hook: "Teen aadmi… ek hi sawaal… aur sirf ek paas hua."
2. Teen dukh: "Rasoolullah ﷺ ne bataya: Bani Israil mein teen aadmi the. Ek kodhi, jisse log door bhaagte. Ek ganja,
   jisse log katraate. Aur ek andha… jo bas logon ko dekhna chahta tha."
3. Pehli mulaqaat: "Allah ne unhe aazmana chaha, aur ek farishta bheja. Usne teeno se ek hi sawaal poochha: tumhe sabse
   zyada kya pasand hai?" / "Kodhi ko saaf rang mila aur ek gyaabhan oontni. Ganje ko baal aur ek gaay. Andhe ko
   aankhen… aur ek bakri. Aur har ek ko dua mili: Allah tumhe isme barkat de."
4. Barkat: "Phir wohi ek oontni, ek gaay, ek bakri… badhte badhte teen poori waadiyan bhar gayin."
5. Imtihaan: "Phir farishta lauta… is baar ek gareeb kodhi musafir ki shakl mein. Usne kaha: mera safar ka saamaan
   khatam ho gaya, Allah ke baad sirf tum ho. Us Allah ke naam par, jisne tumhe yeh rang aur yeh maal diya, ek oont de
   do." / "Jawab mila: mujh par bahut zimmedariyan hain. Kya tum wahi kodhi nahi the? 'Nahi, yeh sab toh baap-dada se
   mila hai.' Ganje ne bhi yahi kaha. Dono se kaha gaya: agar jhoot bolte ho, toh Allah tumhe waisa hi kar de jaisa
   tum the."
6. Teesra darwaza: "Aakhir mein woh ek andhe musafir ki shakl mein teesre ke paas gaya, aur ek bakri maangi." /
   "Usne kaha: main andha tha, Allah ne aankhen di. Gareeb tha, Allah ne ameer banaya. Jo chaho le lo… Allah ke liye jo
   bhi loge, main nahi rokunga."
7. Ant: "Tab jawab aaya: apna maal apne paas rakho. Tum teeno aazmaye gaye the… Allah tumse raazi hua, aur tumhare
   dono saathiyon se naraaz."
   End line: Apna kal mat bhoolo. Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 3464. Comment: Aap teeno mein se kaun hote? Sach batana.

Notes: the Hadith does not say the two were turned back (only the conditional dua + Allah's anger): kept exact.
Which man asked camels vs cows the narrator doubted; we follow the Bukhari text (leper: she-camel, bald: cow).
Tasawwur: village, houses, gates, clothes, faces, the "mirror" memory flashes, people's reactions.
