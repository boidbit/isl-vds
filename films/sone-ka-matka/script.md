# Sone Se Bhara Matka

Format: 9:16 YouTube Shorts · 30–35 sec · Mood: suspense + honesty + emotional twist
Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 3472, Sahih Muslim 1721
Core message: Imaandari aisi bhi hoti hai jo duniya mein bahut kam milti hai.
Note: the judge ("buzurg") is shown only from behind (some scholars say he may have been a Prophet).

## Scene 1 — Hook / Khazana mil gaya! (0–4 sec)
Surroundings: subah, halki sunehri dhoop, purana kheti wala ilaaka, sookhi zameen, patthar, jhaadiyan, door ped, mitti ka ghar.
Action: aadmi zameen khod raha hai, kudal sakht cheez se takrati hai, wo rukta hai, mitti hataata hai.
Camera: close-up kudal ka takrana → extreme close-up haath rukta hai → top angle, mitti hatti hai, matke ka dhakkan.
Narration: "Ek aadmi ne zameen kharidi… lekin khodte waqt use kuch aisa mila, jise dekhkar uski saansein ruk gayin!"
Sound: khudai, Tak!, suspense rise. Screen text: "Zameen ke andar KHAZANA?!"

## Scene 2 — Matka khulta hai (4–8 sec)
Action: matka bahar nikaalta hai, dhool saaf karta hai, kholta hai, sone ki chamak.
Camera: medium close-up front → insert shot andar → gold shine ke saath push-in.
Narration: "Matke ke andar sona hi sona tha…" Sound: lid scrape, shimmer, hawa.

## Scene 3 — Lalach nahi, imaandari (8–13 sec)
Surroundings: gaon ka kachcha rasta, mitti ki deewarein, ghar, bakri, charpai.
Action: matka lekar pehle malik ke ghar, darwaze par pukarta hai, malik bahar aata hai, matka badhata hai.
Camera: wide village lane → over-the-shoulder offer → two-shot.
Narration: "Lekin usne socha… 'Maine tumse zameen kharidi thi, khazana nahi.'" Buyer: "Ye sona aapka hai."

## Scene 4 — Doosra aadmi bhi imaandaar nikla (13–19 sec)
Action: pehla malik haath peeche karke mana karta hai.
Camera: close-up seller → reverse shot buyer surprise → slow zoom.
Narration: "Magar pehle malik ne kaha… 'Maine tumhe zameen bech di thi. Ab jo kuch usme nikla, wo tumhara hai.'"

## Scene 5 — Hairatangez twist (19–26 sec)
Action: dono ek dusre ko matka dene ki koshish karte hain, phir buzurg ke paas jaate hain.
Camera: circular medium shot → buzurg seated, low angle respectful framing.
Narration: "Dono itne imaandaar the… ki sona rakhne ko koi tayyar hi nahi tha!"

## Scene 6 — Beautiful ending (26–35 sec)
Surroundings: golden evening light, village warmth.
Action: Buzurg: "Tumhare beta hai?" "Tumhari beti hai?" Dono haan. Faisla: shaadi aur khazana unhe.
Camera: elder medium → reaction both → slow pull-back wide, sunset.
Narration: "Tab faisla hua — ek ke bete aur doosre ki beti ki shaadi kar di jaye… aur khazana unhe de diya jaye."
Final: "Aisi hoti hai asli imaandari."
Screen text: "Imaandari ka sabse khoobsurat waqia" / "Apne liye nahi… sach ke liye jeena seekho."
